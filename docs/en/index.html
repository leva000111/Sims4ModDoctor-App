Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Ask in our community 💚

Is it safe? Will the app delete anything?

I never delete your mods or saves for good: everything I remove goes into quarantine and comes back with one button. What disappears for good is only what you delete yourself with a button (for example “Delete old ones…” in quarantine), old copies beyond the limit (6 save copies, 2 Mods copies) and the game cache — the game rebuilds it. Before making changes, the app can back up your Mods folder and saves. I don’t change your saves until you press “Restore” yourself (I make a copy first). Exceptions, only on your button: “Households” tidies the Tray folder, and “Why mods aren't visible” can move mod files found in saves or Tray into Mods.

Windows is warning me about the app. What do I do?

The app doesn’t have a digital signature yet, so Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. Click “More info” → “Run anyway”. Only download it from this page or from our channel.

Does the game need to be on this computer?

Yes, the app works with The Sims 4 folder on your computer: Mods, saves, your library. Any version of the game from the EA app or Steam works.

How do I buy the PRO version without crypto?

Click “💎 PRO version” in the app and pick a plan, and an invoice opens in @CryptoBot. In the same place, in the P2P section, you can buy USDT by card with rubles, hryvnias or tenge and pay the invoice. Your activation key arrives in the app automatically.

New computer: will my PRO version carry over?

PRO works on one computer at a time. On the old one, click “💎 PRO version” → “Move to another computer”, then enter your key on the new one. No access to the old computer? Message us in the community and we’ll move it manually.

How do I update the app?

The version from the website updates itself on launch (you can turn this off in Settings); the Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store.

Yes, “🎁 Invite a friend” in the app: for every friend who buys PRO with your link or code, you get +7 days of PRO and your friend gets +7 days (if the friend takes “Lifetime”, only you get the week). Only a friend’s first key counts; purchases from your own computer or your home network (if it has IPv6) don’t count, and from one internet address only the first two in 30 days do.
