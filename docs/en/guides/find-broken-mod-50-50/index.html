How to find a broken mod with the 50/50 method

When the game crashes, loads forever or acts strange and file names don't give the culprit away, there's an old, reliable trick: split your mods in half until only one is left.

Updated: November 25, 2026

When you need this method

  • the game crashes while loading or at a specific moment;
  • the loading screen never ends;
  • something is off: Sims stand frozen, buttons disappear, a menu won't open;
  • you've already pulled script mods and updated everything you could — and the problem is still there.

Why it's fast

Each round rules out half the suspects, so the number of launches grows very slowly:

Files in ModsGame launches to find the culprit
100about 7
1,000about 10
5,000about 13

Before you start

  1. Back up the Mods and Saves folders from Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 4.
  2. Write down how to reproduce the problem: "crashes when loading the Smith household", "the phone won't open". Test the same way every time.
  3. Create two folders on your desktop: "Set aside" and "Checked".

While searching, don't save the game. Load the household, test, and quit without saving — otherwise half your mods will "go missing" from the save.

Step by step

  1. Move half of what's in Mods to "Set aside". Move mods as a whole: a mod's folder, or a creator's .package + .ts4script pair — don't split them up.
  2. Launch the game and test.
  3. Problem still there? The culprit is among the mods still in Mods. The ones you set aside are clean — move them to "Checked".
  4. Problem gone? The culprit is among the ones you set aside. Move what's in Mods to "Checked" and put the set-aside mods back into Mods.
  5. Repeat with the remaining suspects: split in half, test again.
  6. When one suspect is left, you've found the culprit. Move everything from "Checked" back into Mods.

Sometimes the problem is caused by two mods together — a conflict. Then it disappears whichever half you remove. In that case keep the first half in place and split only the second: while the problem persists, the second mod of the pair is still in the game. Once you find it, keep it in and find its partner in the other half the same way.

What to do with the mod you found

  • Check the creator's page: there may already be an update for the current game version.
  • No update yet? Set the mod aside and wait. Creators often fix mods within days of a patch.
  • If the mod is abandoned, look for a similar one by another creator.
  • Tell the creator about the bug: that gets the mod fixed faster for everyone.
